Muz On privacy policy

Muz On plays audio files stored on your device. It has no account system. It uses the internet only to look up missing cover pictures, and you can turn that off in settings.

What it reads. Audio files and their tags, through the audio permission. A folder you choose, through the system folder picker, in order to read a music library database you placed there. Both stay on your device.

What it stores. Your settings, your last queue and copies of cover pictures, in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app removes them.

What it shares. For a track without a picture, and only while "Find missing covers online" is on, the artist name and track title are sent to the Deezer and iTunes search services, along with your IP address as with any internet request. Their own privacy policies apply. Nothing else leaves the device: no analytics, no advertising.

Contact. Questions go to the issue tracker of the project on GitHub.
