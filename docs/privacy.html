Muz On privacy policy

Muz On plays audio files stored on your device. It does not have an account system and does not request internet access, so it cannot send anything off your device.

What it reads. Audio files and their tags, through the audio permission. A folder you choose, through the system folder picker, in order to read a music library database you placed there. Both stay on your device.

What it stores. Your settings, your last queue and small copies of cover pictures, in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app removes them.

What it shares. Nothing. No analytics, no advertising, no third parties.

Contact. Questions go to the issue tracker of the project on GitHub.
